Understand the reference curve.

For compatible formulations, Folumi displays recorded injections in a published model with a fixed research profile. It does not measure drug concentration in your body or personalise the model using your weight.

The curve does not guide doses, treatment or when to inject. Folumi is a record for adults and does not replace care from your healthcare professional.

Your history stays on your device.

The current version works without sign-in or cloud sync. You can export a JSON copy to read outside the app, but it cannot be imported to restore your history.

Your records may be lost if you delete the app or its data, lose your device or move to another device. There is no automatic recovery.
