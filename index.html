An estimated level, clearly labelled.

Folumi draws your estimated medication level from the doses you log and published reference profiles. It is an estimate, not a measurement of the medicine in your body, and you can turn it off. It does not guide doses or tell you when to take them.

Folumi Plus, if you want more.

  • A one-page PDF summary for your appointment. You choose what goes in it.
  • Charts of your weight, doses and how you feel, over any period.
  • How much is left in your box.
  • An encrypted automatic copy, so you can change phones and bring your records.
  • Your next dose on the Lock Screen and in widgets on your Home Screen.

Plus is optional: an annual or monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, through your App Store account. Everything else is free.

Private by design.

No account, no sign-in and no ads. Your records stay on this device, in an encrypted database. If you turn on the automatic copy, it is encrypted on your phone first: not even we can read it. You can also save a copy to a file and restore it on a new phone.

A personal log, not medical advice.

Folumi is for adults. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. It does not recommend doses or tell you when to take them. Talk to your healthcare professional about your treatment.
