Folumi privacy

Updated October 17, 2026

ternmade@outlook.com

Your history on this device

This first version works without an account, sign-in or sync. Injections, schedules, weight, notes, drafts, corrections and preferences stay on your device. Folumi does not send this history to its own server or share it with AI tools, advertisers or social networks.

No automatic recovery

Folumi does not provide backups or recovery on another device. Deleting or reinstalling the app, clearing its data, replacing or losing your device may erase your history. Do not rely on system backups to recover it. The app uses protected storage and excludes its internal files from the backup routines it configures.

The copy you choose to save

Your data lets you export a JSON file containing available records, including recoverable deleted records and your draft, labelled separately. Original values and units are preserved. The file has no password and may contain health information. Choose a trusted destination and control your copies. Export lets you read the file outside the app; this version cannot import it or restore Folumi from it. If you choose a cloud provider in the file picker, that destination processes the copy under its own terms.

Keeping and deleting records

Records remain on the device while you keep the app data. Deleting a record moves it to Deleted records for possible restoration; it does not permanently erase the content. To remove all local app data, delete the app and its data in system settings. Delete exported copies wherever you saved them. There is no remote Folumi account to delete in this version.

Protection and access

The local database is encrypted; its key is held in system-protected storage and tied to this device. Protection also depends on device security. This version does not require a separate Folumi PIN or biometrics: someone with access to your unlocked device can open the app. No system can promise zero risk. Never send passwords, codes or a complete history file to support.

History and the reference curve

This version is intended for adults. Folumi records what you enter; a schedule is not proof of a completed injection. For compatible formulations and amounts, the curve shows recorded injections in fixed research profiles. Recorded weight does not personalise the model. The curve does not measure concentration in your body, predict an individual response or choose a dose, treatment or injection time. Sources, coverage and limitations are available from the curve. The app does not replace a healthcare professional.

Network, external pages and support

You do not need to provide a name, email address or account to use the local history. This composition does not initialise sign-in, behavioural analytics, advertising or a record backend. If you open an external page, your browser and that page provider may process connection information such as IP address and browser agent under their own policies. The static support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, without forms, marketing scripts or cookies added by Folumi.

Responsibility and contact

Arthur Barbosa is the stated person responsible for Folumi, with support through the Ternmade studio. Contact ternmade@outlook.com using the subject Folumi — support or Folumi — privacy. If you contact us, your email provider and ours process the message and sender address to handle the request. We use this correspondence to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations; you may request its deletion using the same contact. Standard support does not require medication, weight or note contents. You may ask about access, correction, deletion and other applicable rights regarding data you send us. We have no remote access to your local history.

Policy changes

This policy describes the local version. Any future account, sync or other use of data requires its own information and controls before activation. Material changes will be presented in the app and on this page with a new date. Store availability is not medical certification.
