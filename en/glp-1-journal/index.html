A printable GLP-1 journal

A blank page for your own entries: doses, times and notes. Print it or keep your history in Folumi.

A record, not a treatment plan

Write down what happened, including the date and time you took the medicine. If you do not remember, leave the field blank or mark it as unknown. An empty row does not mean that a dose was missed.

This page does not tell you what dose to use or when to take it. Follow the instructions you received from your healthcare professional.

Use your browser’s Print menu to print the blank journal or save it as a PDF. This page has no form: nothing you write on paper is sent to Folumi.
