Are my records saved in the cloud?

No, unless you turn on Folumi Plus’s encrypted automatic copy: it is kept off the device, and only your devices can open it. Otherwise, Folumi keeps history on your device without sign-in or sync. To have it on another device, save a copy in Your data and use Restore a copy on the new device.

Are my progress photos in the copy?

No. They stay on this device only, in the notebook’s encrypted database, and are not in the manual or the automatic copy. When you change devices, they do not come along in this version.

Can I restore my history from a copy?

Yes. In Your data, tap Restore a copy and choose the file. Folumi checks the whole file before changing anything and first keeps a copy of what is on the device; if anything fails midway, nothing changes. Do not delete the app to test recovery.

Did switching kg/lb change my original value?

The selected unit changes the display. Original recorded values and units remain preserved; open the record to check them.

Can I use the curve to decide my next dose?

No. It represents a fixed research profile using compatible records, without measuring personal levels. Ask your treating professional about doses, delays or symptoms.

How do I erase my data?

A deleted record stays in the trash and can be restored. To remove all local data, delete the app and its data in system settings. Delete saved files separately. The Plus automatic copy is deleted in Your data, with Delete the stored copy. There is no account to cancel.
