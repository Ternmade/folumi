A checklist for your GLP-1 journal

A short way to review your own records before an appointment, without turning them into treatment advice.

Before reviewing your records

  • Check whether dates refer to when you took the medicine, not just when you wrote the entry.
  • Correct quantities or times you entered incorrectly. Leave what you do not know unspecified.
  • Record changes and pauses you were told about, without rewriting older doses.
  • Add symptoms or questions you want to discuss, with dates when possible.
  • Check units when comparing weight entries.

Before sharing

  • Choose the period and the information you want the other person to see.
  • Review the final file, including personal notes.
  • Keep a separate restorable copy if you need to move your history to another phone. A report and a restorable copy have different purposes.

This is a record-keeping checklist. It is not an injection procedure, a dose calculator or guidance about stopping or restarting treatment.

Get Folumi

No account is needed. Entries are stored in an encrypted database on your phone. If you enable the optional automatic copy, an encrypted copy is sent to storage. Sharing or exporting also sends only the file you choose.
