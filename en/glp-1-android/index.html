A GLP-1 tracker for Android, without an account

Log your doses, weight and notes, and check the schedule you entered. Folumi is available on Google Play.

Start with what you know

  1. Enter the medication and schedule you were given. Setting a schedule does not create past doses.
  2. Log a dose you actually took. You can correct its date, time or quantity later.
  3. Review your notebook and the last 14 days. Keep a copy you can restore.

Move your history

Preview a Shotsy CSV or JSON export before importing. On Android, you can also import weight entries from Health Connect with your permission. Importing does not recreate information absent from the source.

Free journal, optional Plus

Dose logging, the schedule, weight, notes and the 14-day summary are free. PDF reports, longer-period charts, inventory, widgets and an automatic encrypted copy belong to Folumi Plus. The current offer and localized prices are shown in the app before purchase.

Get Folumi

No account is needed. Entries are stored in an encrypted database on your phone. If you enable the optional automatic copy, an encrypted copy is sent to storage. Sharing or exporting also sends only the file you choose.
